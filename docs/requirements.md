# Requirements (approved)

## Must-have
1. Checkout: barcode scan, search, quick buttons, cart with bottle/carton/tot, cash + M-Pesa, receipt print, cash drawer trigger
2. Product catalogue: SKU, barcode, category, buying/selling price, pack sizes (carton, bottle, tots)
3. Stock: live levels, goods received, stock take, adjustments with reasons, automatic carton/bottle/tot depletion
4. Till/shift: open/close, expected vs counted cash, expected vs received M-Pesa
5. Roles: cashier and owner, PIN login; owner controls voids, discounts, price changes
6. Owner dashboard: sales, top sellers, low stock/reorder, variance, void/discount log
7. Offline mode and eTIMS-ready receipts

## Should-have
Suppliers and purchase orders, Excel opening-stock import, daily WhatsApp/SMS summary to owner, 18+ prompt

## Later
Customer credit, card payments, live eTIMS integration, multi-outlet, accounting export, loyalty
