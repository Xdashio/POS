# Prototype vs. production product

The [Cellar POS prototype](../prototype/index.html) is a clickable, in-memory demonstration. It shows proposed workflows and seeded sample data; it is not connected to a payment provider, printer, cash drawer, tax service, or production database. A production build should turn these flows into durable, recoverable operations and validate the final design with the shop’s real data and equipment.

| Owner pain point | What the prototype demonstrates | What is simulated or limited | What the production build should deliver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shrinkage** | Stock is derived from a movement ledger. Sales, receipts, adjustments, voids, and stock takes change the displayed quantity. Stock-take variances appear in Reports; reasoned adjustments and protected actions appear in the audit log. | Data lives in browser memory and resets on reload. Seeded products and movements are examples. A count records what staff enter; the prototype cannot verify what is physically on the shelf or identify theft by itself. | Persist each movement reliably, including during outages, and sync it idempotently so retries cannot double-count. Keep a searchable, attributable audit history; enforce owner permissions for sensitive actions; support repeatable stock counts, variance review, and alerts using the client’s agreed thresholds. |
| **Cash / M-Pesa mismatch** | Till open/close compares expected cash with denomination counts and expected M-Pesa with the amount received. A variance reason and owner sign-off are retained with the shift receipt. Offline M-Pesa codes can be compared manually with imported statement rows and marked matched or disputed. | STK push, connection state, sync, and statement data are mocked. No Safaricom, bank, or settlement feed is connected, so the prototype does not confirm that money moved. | Integrate the agreed M-Pesa merchant setup and handle callbacks and transaction references safely. Store till sessions and reconciliation decisions durably, prevent duplicate posting, support the client’s statement format, and make unresolved differences visible to owners. |
| **Reordering** | The reorder list estimates carton quantities from recent sales velocity, configured minimums, and a 14-day cover target. It groups suggestions by supplier and can build a draft, preview WhatsApp text, and export CSV. | The calculation uses the demo’s available sales history and product settings. Supplier contact, lead time, minimum order quantity, and seasonality are not fully modeled. WhatsApp and CSV actions are previews/simulations; no supplier receives an order. | Load the shop’s real catalog and supplier terms. Agree and tune the replenishment rules, including lead time, case sizes, minimums, and seasonal products. Provide an approved purchase-order workflow with status, history, and the chosen real export or messaging integration. |
| **Slow checkout** | Product search, tap-friendly tiles, a random-product scan simulator, bottle/carton/tot quantities, keyboard shortcuts, hold/recall, discounts, cash and M-Pesa screens, and a receipt preview demonstrate the intended fast path. | The scan simulator picks a random catalog item. The browser-based STK flow, offline queue, printer, and cash-drawer tests are simulated. Data is not durable offline and no checkout latency target has been validated with shop staff or devices. | Deliver a responsive checkout backed by a durable local store and queued sync. Support the actual barcode scanner, printer, and drawer models, integrate the agreed payment flow, preserve completed sales through power/network loss, and test checkout speed with real staff and product data. |

## Boundary of this demo

- CSV export and WhatsApp sharing are previews; they do not send or submit orders.
- The eTIMS receipt shows placeholder fields and a QR placeholder. It is not a KRA submission or a compliance claim.
- Printer and drawer checks report simulated results. Confirm hardware models and operating systems before selecting drivers or connection methods.
- VAT settings control the prototype receipt display. Confirm registration and tax treatment with the client before implementing production tax logic.
- The prototype is a workflow reference. Production storage, security, integrations, backups, monitoring, and deployment still need to be built and tested.
